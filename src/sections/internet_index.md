*Internet Index* is a digital and physical publication launching in late 2024. It archives the Internet in its historical, political and social context through interviews, essays,  data analysis and visualizations, interdisciplinary research projects, artworks and more. 

Visit [internetindex.net](http://internetindex.net) for updates.
