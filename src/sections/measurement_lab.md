Measurement Lab (M-Lab), most well known for providing the "speed" test embedded in [Google Search](https://www.google.com/search?q=how+fast+is+my+internet&oq=how+fast+is+my+internet), collects and publishes Internet measurement data for public use. 

As **Director** (2019 - 2022), I led the strategic and operational initiatives of the organization and as **Research and Data Lead** (2022 -    current) I support and advise upon the use of Internet measurement data in policy, advocacy, governance, academic and industry research and more.

You can read more about M-Lab's work on [measurementlab.net](https://measurementlab.net).
