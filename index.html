🪩 Lai Yi Ohlsen 🪩

Artist, “technologist”, researcher, adjunct lecturer.
New York, NY.

Themes

Internet infrastructure, measeurement, data, gaps in data, heligographs, mirrors, network topologies, geopolitics, information structures, relationality, poetics of code, choreographic systems, video essays, storage containers, improvisation, diagrams, basketball, the Phoenix Suns, the New York Knicks, etc.

Current

‣ Acting Director & Data Scientist at Measurement Lab studying the evolution of Internet Performance. Measurement Lab’s mission is to measure the Internet, save the data and make it universally accessible and useful. We publish our data for use by those who improve the Internet including academic researchers, policymakers and network operators. Measurement Lab is a fiscally sponsored project of Code for Science & Society.
‣ Analytics for a Better World Fellow building Data Science skills for use in the non-profit sector.
‣ Part-time Lecturer for the New School's Parsons Design & Technology Program teaching Critical Computation to Fall 2022 BFA and MFA cohorts.
‣ Latest visual artwork "the Internet" up for viewing at Public Works Administration as a Year 8 New Inc member & Art+Code Track, in collaboration with Rhizome. The exhibition was organized by Celine Katzman and features works by artists Stephen Kwok, Yehwan Song, Woody Sullender, Cassie Tarakajian, Roopa Vasudevan, Carrie Sijia Wang, and Rosalie Yu.

Less Current

‣ Dancer and collaborator in Benjamin Akio Kimitch’s Tiger Hands, a “cosmic, sunrise-colored search for authentic personal expression, using elements of Peking opera as a transformative source of beauty and strength.” The show premiered in August 2022 at the Shed. A review was published in Brooklyn Rail.
‣ 2019 Movement Research Artist-in-Residence, a 2019 Chez Bushwick Artist-in-Residence, a 2020 Pioneer Works Technology Resident and selected paticipant in the 2017 Triple Canopy Publication Intensive.
‣ Panelist and presenter on behalf of Measurement Lab at Next Century Cities' Bipartisan Tech Conference, TPRC, RIPE, RightsCon, CSV Conf and more.
‣ Published essay titled "Measuring the Internet as a Gesture of Tending (Towards)" in the Are.Na Annual 202, themed "tend".
‣ Presented visual, print and choregraphic work at the New York Tech Zine Fair, Movement Research’s Fall Festival, New York Art Book Fair, the Internet Archive’s Decentralized Web Summit and Our Networks.
‣ Technical Program Manager at equalit.ie working on Deflect, a DDOS mitigation tool for human rights and media organizations and CENO a censorship circumvention application using peer-to-peer web technologies.
